About Us

Building the Future of Education

FusionZone was founded with a simple mission — make quality education accessible, engaging, and fun for every child in India.

Our Mission

To provide world-class digital education to children from pre-primary to secondary level, bridging the gap between traditional and modern learning.

Our Vision

A world where every child, regardless of location or background, has access to engaging, high-quality educational content.

Our Story

FusionZone started in 2020 when a group of educators and technologists came together during the pandemic to solve a critical problem — how do we keep children engaged in learning when schools are closed?

What began as a small YouTube channel with free video lessons for primary classes has grown into India's most comprehensive child education platform, serving over 50,000 students across 12 class levels.

Our team of 50+ experienced teachers, content creators, and developers work tirelessly to create content that is not just educational but truly delightful for young learners.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2025

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for the Platform to function properly. These include session cookies, authentication cookies, and security cookies. They cannot be disabled.

Performance Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with our Platform by collecting anonymous data. We use Google Analytics for this purpose.

Functionality Cookies

Remember your preferences and choices (like language, class selection, and theme) to provide a more personalized experience.

Marketing Cookies

Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness. These are set with your consent only.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use cookies from trusted third parties: Google Analytics (performance), YouTube (video embedding), and payment processors. Each third party has their own cookie policy.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Note that disabling cookies may affect Platform functionality. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

For cookie-related queries, email privacy@FusionZone.com.
